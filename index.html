Flagship · Mulazim

Private AI for government and industry.

Mulazim is an AI platform installed inside your organisation. It works from your own databases and documents, cites the source behind every answer, and can run fully offline. Three products are built on it.

Mulazim AI

Answers from your own data, with the source.

Ask in plain language. Every figure and quote is traced back to the query or page it came from.

Public chatbots have never seen your records, and anything you paste into them leaves your control. Mulazim connects to your systems inside your network, reasons over the data and writes up what it finds.

  • Data analyst. Plain-language questions over your ERP and databases, answered with the numbers behind them.
  • Document answers. Search across laws, rules, policies and files, with the clause or page cited.
  • Reports and decks. Findings written up as reports, spreadsheets and slide decks you can edit.
  • Video analysis. Mission Control counts and tracks what appears in recorded or live camera footage, processed on site.

Mulazim Studio

An engineering team of AI specialists.

Give it a brief and reference designs. Get blueprints, CAD models, analysis and renders, reviewed at every step.

Studio plans the work as a graph of specialist roles, from body design and chassis to propulsion and homologation, and runs it with real engineering tools. Every step stops at an approval gate, and you decide which gates need an engineer's sign-off.

  • 59 specialist roles. Automotive, armoured vehicle and drone engineering, picked per project.
  • Real outputs. STEP, STL and 3D models, FEA, renders and dimensioned drawings.
  • Human gates. Approve or send back with comments. Open issues need a written waiver.
  • Your materials. Designs use your own catalogue of materials and processes, ranked by availability.

Assembly OS

Sixteen applications for the whole Assembly.

Purpose-built for a legislative assembly: its members, officers and staff, each seeing only what their role allows.

Assembly OS works from the Assembly's own rules, records and proceedings, in Urdu, Sindhi and English, by voice or text. It runs inside the Assembly, can work without the internet, and keeps an audit record of every action.

  • Bills, start to finish. Research, first draft and a legal check against existing law, for the Law branch to finalise.
  • Questions and replies. Sorted by subject, sent to the right minister, tracked until answered.
  • Paper to digital. Old files scanned into a permanent, searchable archive.

Mulazim · Deployment

Runs where your data lives.

Mulazim is installed in your environment, not rented from ours. Choose how it generates answers. You can switch later without rebuilding anything.

On-premisesFully offline

Blackbox

The whole platform, including the AI models, runs on your own GPU server. It can work with no internet connection at all.

Leaves your building
Nothing. It can run air-gapped.
Hardware

Your servers + hosted modelNo GPU needed

Networked

The platform runs on your servers and sends each request to a hosted model you choose, such as Claude or DeepSeek, to write the answer. Search, storage, speech and access control stay on your side.
