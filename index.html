Mulazim Studio

An engineering team of AI specialists.

Give it a brief and reference designs. Get blueprints, CAD models, analysis and renders, reviewed at every step.

Studio plans the work as a graph of specialist roles, from body design and chassis to propulsion and homologation, and runs it with real engineering tools. Nothing moves forward until an engineer approves it.

  • 59 specialist roles. Automotive, armoured vehicle and drone engineering, picked per project.
  • Real outputs. STEP, STL and 3D models, FEA, renders and dimensioned drawings.
  • Human gates. Approve or send back with comments. Open issues need a written waiver.
  • Your materials. Designs use your own catalogue of materials and processes, ranked by availability.

Assembly OS

Sixteen applications. One platform.

Purpose-built for a legislative assembly: its members, officers and staff, each seeing only what their role allows.

Assembly OS learns from the Assembly's own rules, records and proceedings, and works in Urdu, Sindhi and English by voice or text. It runs inside the Assembly, without the internet, and records every action.

  • Bills, start to finish. Research, first draft and a legal check against existing law, for the Law branch to finalise.
  • Questions and replies. Sorted by subject, sent to the right minister, tracked until answered.
  • Paper to digital. Old files scanned into a permanent, searchable archive.
