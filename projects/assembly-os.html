Assembly OS

Sixteen applications for a legislative assembly, for its members, officers and staff, each seeing only what their role allows.

Overview

Assembly OS works from the assembly's own rules, records and proceedings. It runs inside the assembly, can work without the internet, and keeps an audit record of every action.

What it does

  • Bills, start to finish

    Research, a first draft and a legal check against existing law, for the Law branch to finalise.

  • Questions and replies

    Members' questions sorted by subject, sent to the right minister and tracked until answered.

  • Approvals and dashboards

    Files arrive summarised, ready to decide, and the whole assembly is visible at a glance.

  • Proceedings journal

    A searchable record of every sitting.

  • Translator and summariser

    Any document explained or translated in Urdu, Sindhi or English, by voice or text.

  • Paper to digital

    Old files scanned into a permanent, searchable archive.

Who uses what

  • Members and executives

    Bill drafting and scrutiny, proceedings journal, approvals, dashboards and reports, meetings and schedule.

  • Officers

    Rules and precedents, questions and replies, drafting assistant, correspondence, deadlines and alerts, digital records.

  • Everyone

    Translator, document summariser, staff helpdesk, HR self-service and smart search.
