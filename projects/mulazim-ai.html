Mulazim AI

Private AI that answers from your organisation's own databases and documents, and cites the source behind every answer.

Overview

Public chatbots have never seen your records, and anything pasted into them leaves your control. Mulazim is installed inside your organisation, connects to your systems and answers questions from your own data, showing where every figure and quote came from.

What it does

  • Data analyst

    Ask in plain language. It queries your ERP and databases and shows the numbers behind the answer.

  • Document answers

    Search across laws, rules, policies and files, with the clause or page cited.

  • Reports and decks

    Findings written up as reports, spreadsheets and slide decks you can edit.

  • Video analysis

    Counts and tracks what appears in recorded or live camera footage, processed on site.

  • Read-only by design

    It reaches your databases through a read-only account. The database itself refuses any change.

  • Access by role

    Each person sees only what their role allows. Database passwords and keys never reach the AI.

How it works

  1. 1

    Install

    Mulazim is installed on your servers and connected to your databases and document stores.

  2. 2

    Ask

    Staff ask questions in plain language, by chat.

  3. 3

    Answer with sources

    Mulazim plans the queries, reads the results and writes the answer, citing the rows and pages it used.

Two ways to run it

  • Blackbox

    The whole platform, including the AI models, runs on your own GPU server. It can work with no internet connection at all.

  • Networked

    Runs on your servers and sends each question, with the passages or rows used to answer it, to a hosted model you choose.

Built with

  • Rust
  • Qdrant
  • Postgres
  • Next.js
  • Local or hosted LLMs
