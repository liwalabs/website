Mulazim Studio

A team of AI engineering specialists that takes a product brief to blueprints, CAD models, analysis and renders, with an approval gate at every step.

Overview

Give Studio a brief and reference designs. It plans the work as a graph of specialist roles, runs each one with real engineering tools, and stops for an engineer's sign-off before anything moves on.

What it does

  • Plans the work

    Reads the brief, sets the goal, researches and plans which specialist roles run, in what order.

  • Specialist roles

    Body design, chassis, powertrain, aerodynamics, cost and homologation, picked per project.

  • Real engineering output

    STEP and STL files, 3D models, FEA, renders and dimensioned drawings.

  • Approval gates

    Every step stops for sign-off. Send it back with comments and that role reworks it.

  • Checked numbers

    Calculated figures must cite the tool run that produced them. Open review findings need a written waiver.

  • Your materials

    Designs use your own catalogue of materials and processes, ranked by availability.

How it works

  1. 1

    Brief

    You give the brief, reference designs and any existing models.

  2. 2

    Plan

    Studio interprets the brief, sets the goal, researches and plans the graph of roles.

  3. 3

    Build and review

    Each role produces its part. You approve it, or send it back with comments.
