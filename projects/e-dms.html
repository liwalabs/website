e-DMS

Capture, text search, filing and sharing of a department's paper documents, on the department's own server.

Overview

Officers kept paper, and photos of paper, on their own phones and PCs. Nothing was searchable or backed up, and when a person left, their documents left with them. e-DMS puts every captured document on the server, with text that can be searched.

What it does

  • Capture anywhere

    Phone camera or PC upload, including existing images and PDFs. Captures made without a connection wait on the phone and upload later.

  • Searchable text

    Text is read on the department's server, never in the cloud. Every scan becomes a searchable PDF, and the text can be corrected.

  • Folders and search

    Each officer files documents in their own folders and finds them by name or by what they say.

  • Personal stays personal

    A personal document is seen only by its owner and the people they share it with, not even by an administrator.

  • Handover built in

    When an officer moves on, the administrator transfers their official documents to the successor.

  • Originals never edited

    The original file is stored once and served as stored. Nothing overwrites it.

How it works

  1. 1

    Capture

    Photograph the page with a phone, or upload it from a PC.

  2. 2

    Read

    The server reads the text and makes a searchable PDF.

  3. 3

    File and find

    File it in a folder, then find it later by any word it contains.

Built with

  • Fastify
  • React, installable app
  • SQLite or Postgres
  • Tesseract OCR
